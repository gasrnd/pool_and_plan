Pool & Plan

Connect your Todoist account to see your tasks here.

  1. Open Todoist settings → Integrations → Developer.
  2. Copy your API token and paste it below.

The token is kept in this browser only. Anyone who has it can read and change your Todoist, so don't share it. If your browser clears site data when it closes, let your password manager save it: the account name is the username and the token is the password. You can add more Todoist accounts later from the \u22ef menu.
